About us

Our garden

North Sydney Community Garden is run by volunteers and has about 40 members.

The committee wants to welcome more younger members and families.

Our members

Most members are around 60 or older. About 10% are under 40.

The garden holds a working bee on the first Saturday of every month.

Everyone is welcome to come along.
